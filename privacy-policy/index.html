Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 13, 2026

EzyAgric ("we", "our", "us") values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your personal information when you use our website, mobile applications, and related services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

  • Identification and contact details such as your name, phone number, email address, and location.
  • Farm and business information, including crop details, farm records, and transaction history.
  • Device and technical information such as IP address, browser type, operating system, and usage logs.
  • Communication data from customer support interactions, surveys, and feedback.

2. How We Use Information

We use personal information to:

  • Provide, manage, and improve our services.
  • Process transactions and deliver products and advisory services.
  • Communicate important updates, service notices, and support responses.
  • Enhance platform security, prevent fraud, and enforce our terms.
  • Comply with legal and regulatory obligations.

3. Sharing of Information

We may share information with trusted service providers, business partners, and affiliates who support service delivery. We may also disclose information where required by law, court order, or lawful request.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

5. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

6. Your Rights and Choices

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information. You may also object to or restrict certain processing activities.

7. Third-Party Services

Our services may contain links to third-party websites or tools. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those third parties.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for children without guardian supervision. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

9. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

10. Contact Us

For questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, contact us at: info@ezyagric.com.
